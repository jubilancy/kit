#### Art Res.
- [Dynamic poses](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/7852) A detailed blog article written by Cheishiru about drawing and breaking down dynamic perspective of the body.
- [Quickposes.com](https://quickposes.com/) Popular website offering poses for gesture drawing, improving speed and setting challenges.
- [Line of Action](https://line-of-action.com/index.php) provides reference photos and practice tools for visual artists
- [Jookpubstock.com](https://jookpubstock.com/) Another great, big reference website. Includes textures, nature, objects and animals too as well as indivitual body parts.
- [Justsketch.me](https://app.justsketch.me/) very simple posing tool. I don't really use 3D tools for art but they're still fun to mess around with
- [Perspective Grid](https://www.reubenlara.com/perspectivegrid/) Create a perspective grid/ guidelines for your drawings.
