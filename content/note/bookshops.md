[Randomness Guide to London: bookshops category](https://london.randomness.org.uk/wiki.cgi?Category_Bookshops)
- Made by the same people as this site, with writeups that go into more depth. Not comprehensive; only covers the bookshops we've actually been to.
[londonbooks.tumblr.com](http://londonbooks.tumblr.com/)
- Photos and brief writeups of London's most interesting bookshops.
[Londonist: Biblio-Text](http://londonist.com/tags/biblio-text)
- A series of articles on London's independent bookshops.
[The (other) London Bookshop Map](https://www.thelondonbookshopmap.org/)
- Map showing 100 independent bookshops in London, with brief writeups. Differs from this site in that it's selective rather than comprehensive. Also available in paper form.
