## Core Boolean Operators

- **"Exact phrase"** — Search for an exact match.
    
    Example: **`"chief marketing officer"`**
    
- **(minus)** — Exclude words or domains from results.
    
    Example: **`marketing -agency`**
    
- **OR** — Include results for either term.
    
    Example: **`"influencer" OR "brand ambassador"`**
    
- **( )** — Group ideas or terms together.
    
    Example: **`("CEO" OR "Founder") ("EdTech" OR "SaaS")`**
    
- — Wildcard operator (unknown words).
    
    Example: **`"Head of * Department"`**
    

## Advanced Google Search Operators

- **site:** — Limit search to a specific domain.
    
    Example: **`site:linkedin.com "business development director"`**
    
- **filetype:** — Search for files by extension: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, CSV.
    
    Example: **`filetype:pdf "annual report" "Tesla"`**
    
- **intitle:** — Find results with a keyword in the page title.
    
    Example: **`intitle:"research paper" "nanotechnology"`**
    
- **inurl:** — Match results with keywords in the page URL.
    
    Example: **`inurl:contacts "marketing"`**
    
- **AROUND(X):** — Locate words appearing close to each other.
    
    Example: **`"Elon Musk" AROUND(5) "SpaceX"`**
    
- **before:/after:** — Filter results by publication date.
    
    Example: **`"AI ethics" after:2023 before:2025`**

## Expert-Level Operators and Combinations

- **related:** — Show sites similar to a given domain.
    
    Example: **`related:nytimes.com`**
    
- **cache:** — See Google’s cached version of a page.
    
    Example: **`cache:bbc.com`**
    
- **define:** — Quickly find word definitions.
    
    Example: **`define:blockchain`**
    
- **source:** — Use with Google News to limit results to a publication.
    
    Example: **`"quantum computing" source:nature`**
    
- **inanchor:** — Find pages that link using specific anchor text.
    
    Example: **`inanchor:"download whitepaper"`**
    
- **link:** — Show pages linking to a specific URL (limited support).
    
    Example: **`link:harvard.edu`**
    
- **location:** — Geographically focus results (used mostly in news searches).
    
    Example: **`"CEO interview" location:london`**
    

## Pro-Search Stacking Examples

- To **find decision-maker emails**:
    
    `textsite:linkedin.com/in "marketing director" "@gmail.com" OR "@company.com"`
    
- To **download research papers legally**:
    
    `textfiletype:pdf "machine learning" site:researchgate.net OR site:arxiv.org`
    
- To **discover competitor documents**:
    
    `textsite:competitor.com filetype:xlsx "pricing" OR "budget"`
    
- To **pull verified leads**:
    
    `text("CEO" OR "Founder") "contact" "email" site:linkedin.com OR site:crunchbase.com`
    
- To **find hidden public files online**:
    
    `text(filetype:xls OR filetype:csv OR filetype:pdf) "confidential" OR "internal use"`
