# Web Clipping & Tweet Saving

Saving tweets and web content into your vault is essential for research and curation. Here's the toolkit that actually works.

---

## Recommended Tools

| Tool | Best For | How It Works |
|------|----------|-------------|
| [SingleFile](https://www.singlefile.org/) | Tweets & threads | Browser extension (Firefox/Chrome) that saves entire webpages as self-contained HTML |
| [Obsidian Web Clipper](https://obsidian.md/clipper) | General web content | Official Obsidian extension; captures and clips directly into your vault |
| Screenshots | Quick saves | Fast for tweets; less structured but no setup required |

---

## How to Use SingleFile

SingleFile is the community favorite for capturing tweets and threads:

1. **Install** — Add extension from [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/single-file/) or [Chrome](https://chromestore.google.com/detail/single-file/mpiodmgfkpcdbccfiveicnfalbmklbfl) store
2. **Capture** — Right-click → "Save page with SingleFile"
3. **Import to Obsidian** — Toggle HTML rendering in Obsidian to view the saved page

The saved HTML is self-contained (images, styles, everything included), so you can archive tweets permanently without worrying about deleted accounts or dead links.

---

## Obsidian Integration

**Built-in option:**  
Use the official [Obsidian Web Clipper](https://obsidian.md/clipper) for streamlined capture directly into your vault with formatting intact.

**For HTML files:**  
Obsidian's HTML toggle lets you preview SingleFile captures without converting them first.

---

## When to Use Each

- **SingleFile** → Entire threads, complex layouts, offline preservation
- **Obsidian Web Clipper** → Quick clips with formatted text and links
- **Screenshots** → One-off tweets or quick references

---

## Why This Matters

Dead links and deleted tweets disappear. Capturing them as HTML keeps your sources intact forever—critical for research, curation, and building a reliable knowledge base.
