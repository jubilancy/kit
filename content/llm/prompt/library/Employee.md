## Email & Communication {#email-&-communication}

### 1\. Cold Outreach Email That Actually Gets Replies

**Prompt:**

```
Write a cold outreach email from me to [prospect name/title] at [company name]. I run [your business — one sentence description]. I want to offer them [your service/product]. The email should be under 120 words, open with something specific about their company (use this detail: [something you noticed about them — a recent launch, blog post, hiring page, etc.]), clearly state what I do and why it matters to them, and end with a low-friction CTA like booking a 15-minute call. Tone: confident but not salesy. No fluff, no "I hope this email finds you well."
```

---

### 2\. Follow-Up Email Sequence (3 Touches)

**Prompt:**

```
Write a 3-email follow-up sequence for a prospect who hasn't responded to my initial outreach. Context: I originally pitched them on [what you pitched]. Space the emails 3 days, 5 days, and 10 days apart. Email 1 should be a short nudge with a new angle or added value. Email 2 should share a quick case study or result: [paste a result or testimonial you've gotten]. Email 3 should be a polite breakup email that leaves the door open. Keep each under 80 words. Subject lines included.
```

### 3\. Client Response to a Scope Creep Request

**Prompt:**

```
Write a professional but firm email response to a client who just asked me to do [describe the extra work they requested] which is outside our original agreement of [describe original scope]. I want to maintain the relationship but set a clear boundary. Offer two options: (1) we add it to scope at an additional cost of [your price], or (2) we table it for a Phase 2. Tone: warm, professional, zero pushover energy.
```

